Question: What was our total recognized revenue in USD for paid invoices in 2024?
Status: ok
Explanation: Sums USD amounts from recognized revenue invoices dated in 2024, showing the paid total, the refund total, and the net revenue as separate columns.
SQL:
SELECT ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN status = 'paid' THEN amount_usd ELSE 0 END), 2) AS paid_revenue_usd, ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN status = 'refunded' THEN amount_usd ELSE 0 END), 2) AS refunds_usd, ROUND(SUM(amount_usd), 2) AS net_revenue_usd FROM v_recognized_revenue WHERE invoice_date >= DATE '2024-01-01' AND invoice_date < DATE '2025-01-01'

Question: What's the churn rate among Enterprise accounts vs Starter?
Status: ok
Explanation: Computes the share of accounts flagged as churned, along with total and churned account counts, for the Enterprise and Starter plans using the one-row-per-account view.
SQL:
SELECT plan, COUNT(*) AS total_accounts, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE churned) AS churned_accounts, ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE churned) / COUNT(*), 2) AS churn_rate_pct FROM v_accounts WHERE plan IN ('Enterprise', 'Starter') GROUP BY plan ORDER BY plan

Question: List the top 5 accounts by revenue, and flag any with CSAT ≤ 2.
Status: ok
Explanation: Ranks accounts by net recognized revenue in USD (paid plus negative refunds, shown as separate columns), returns the top five, and flags those whose CSAT score is 2 or lower.

WITH rev AS (SELECT account_id, SUM(amount_usd) FILTER (WHERE status = 'paid') AS paid_usd, SUM(amount_usd) FILTER (WHERE status = 'refunded') AS refunds_usd, SUM(amount_usd) AS net_usd FROM v_recognized_revenue GROUP BY account_id) SELECT a.account_id, a.account_name, ROUND(COALESCE(r.paid_usd, 0), 2) AS paid_usd, ROUND(COALESCE(r.refunds_usd, 0), 2) AS refunds_usd, ROUND(r.net_usd, 2) AS net_revenue_usd, a.csat_score, COALESCE(a.csat_score <= 2, FALSE) AS low_csat_flag FROM rev r JOIN v_accounts a ON a.account_id = r.account_id ORDER BY r.net_usd DESC LIMIT 5
